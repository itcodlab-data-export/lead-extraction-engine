## What it does

Runs on a daily schedule, reads 14 public intent sources, deduplicates against a master index and writes verified business contacts to a five-tab, 24-column Google Sheet.

## Principles

- Extraction only. It does no sending and no copywriting.
- No CSV files are produced, and contact details are masked in logs.
- If one source fails, the rest of the run continues.
