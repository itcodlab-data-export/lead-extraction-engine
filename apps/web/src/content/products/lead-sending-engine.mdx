## Status

In development and not yet available. This page describes the design intent only.

## Design principles

- Nine independent safety layers sit between a send and your domain reputation.
- Daily ceilings are fixed in code. Configuration may tighten them but never loosen them.
- If a safety counter cannot be read, sending is skipped (fail-closed).
